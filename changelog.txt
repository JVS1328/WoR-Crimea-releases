Crimean War 1.1.2 - weapon, artillery and Onslaught fixes

- Littikh rifle: the sling shows its own leather strap again (it had the wrong texture, striped like dirt); the barrel is a clean browned service finish instead of a pitted museum piece; the rear sight's notch is opened up, so the front sight sits visibly in it at every range.
- Carabine a tige and Victoria carbine: the same clean barrels, no speckle.
- Russian M1828 musket: the rear sight's notch is opened up (it was a slit).
- French Mle 1842 musket: the front sight now sits in the rear notch; it had been cut so low that the barrel bands filled the notch.
- Deane-Adams revolver: the hammer now moves when you fire.
- French cavalry pistol and Russian officer's pistol: held in the hand like the stock revolvers (the hand had gripped the bottom of the butt); sights re-aimed.
- Cannons: the sight seat at the breech is a proper screwed plate (it showed as a striped, broken block); every gun has its front sight: a pyramid on the muzzle on the Russian pieces, the notch in the muzzle swell on the French canon-obusier. Laying re-measured.
- Russian infantry: the cap pouch on the chest belt faces the right way, sits on the belt and is smaller, as in period paintings.
- Onslaught loading screens redone to match the game's own: old damaged photographs, fog and smoke, the dead running at the camera; the Traktir bridge, the siege camp and the Mackenzie heights. Harpers Ferry (Balaklava) shows the game's own screen for now.
- Still client-side only: you can join normal servers, and other players see the vanilla game.

Built for the War of Rights patch of October 28, 2026 (Steam build 26270438).
