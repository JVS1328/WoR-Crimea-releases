Crimean War 1.2.4 - Portraits with their coats

- Regiment selection: the Moscow, Tobolsk, Praga, Poltava, Olonets, Tomsk and Okhotsk Regiments' portraits showed some men without a coat (the belts, pack and trousers with nothing between them). They now wear their greatcoats, coats or shirts as in game, and their drummers, buglers and fifers have portraits of their own.
- Still client-side only: you can join normal servers, and other players see the vanilla game.

Built for the War of Rights patch of November 4, 2026 (Steam build 26426403).
