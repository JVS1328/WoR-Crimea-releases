Crimean War 1.1.7 - Artillery sights and the November 4 game patch

- 10-pdr Parrott (the Russian quarter-pood unicorn): the sight pad on the breech hid the front sight, so the gun could not be aimed over its sights and shots went high. The pad is lower now, and the barrel is laid so the front sight's point sits exactly where the stock Parrott's blade aims.
- 3-inch Ordnance rifle (the Russian 6-pounder): the front sight sat a little high, so shots fell low. It now sits exactly where the stock 3-inch's blade aims.
- The 12-pounders are unchanged.
- Updated for the November 4 game patch: its new short-sword movement animations play with the mod installed.
- Still client-side only: you can join normal servers, and other players see the vanilla game.
